ABOUT THE BOOK

Not Every Kid Goes Pro is built around a simple idea: youth sports matter even when the athlete never reaches the next level. Coaching can still teach confidence, work ethic, teamwork, resilience and how to handle hard days.

WHO IT'S FOR

Coaches, parents and anyone trying to keep youth sports competitive without losing sight of the kid inside the uniform.
